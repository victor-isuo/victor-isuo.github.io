How I work

What you're actually getting.

Not a freelancer who disappears after delivery. An engineer who thinks about your problem before writing a line of code.

01

Understand the real constraint

Most AI projects fail because the engineering solution is built before the actual problem is understood. I spend time on the constraint first — cost, latency, data quality, reliability — before touching architecture. That usually saves three weeks of rebuilding.

02

Build with explainability in mind

Every design decision I make has a reason I can state out loud. If you ask me why I used CrewAI over a single chain for LexAI, I have a specific answer about sequential task decomposition and schema-constrained handoffs. That's not just good engineering — it's what you need when a skeptical stakeholder asks.

03

Ship something deployable

Everything I build is containerized, version-controlled, and documented. Not because I'm tidy — because I've seen enough "AI projects" die in notebooks on someone's laptop. The system should run the same on your infrastructure as it does on mine.
