AgentEval
Most teams don't know when their AI system breaks — they find out from users, too late. AgentEval turns evaluation into a first-class system layer — automatically scoring outputs, tracking quality over time, and blocking deployments when performance regresses.

The evaluation layer built for the Industrial AI Copilot — and deployable for any LLM system. Runs automated test suites across three evaluation modes (RAG pipeline, single-agent, multi-agent) using custom LLM-as-judge scoring — not RAGAS. Why this matters Silent quality regression is the #1 way production AI systems erode trust. Without automated evaluation, teams only discover degradation after users do — by which point the damage is done. AgentEval closes that gap: every push is scored, every regression is blocked before deployment, every quality trend is tracked over time.

Production Reality
What Breaks in Production AI Systems

Most failures don't come from the model.
They come from everything around it.

⚠ 01
Retrieval looks correct — but misses critical constraints. The right document type surfaces, but the constraint that matters is buried or filtered out.
⚠ 02
Agents pass tests — but fail in edge cases. Eval suites cover happy paths. Production exposes the paths you didn't write tests for.
⚠ 03
Prompt changes introduce regressions — nobody tracks them. A well-intentioned prompt update drops accuracy 8% across a category. No one notices for weeks.
⚠ 04
Outputs sound confident — but cannot be audited. The model gives an answer. There's no trail back to why. You can't debug what you can't trace.
⚠ 05
Multi-agent systems produce answers — but no one knows which agent was wrong. When the supervisor returns a bad result, you have no visibility into which specialist caused it.
These are not theoretical problems.
They are how production AI systems lose trust — slowly, then all at once.

I build systems where these failures are visible, measurable, and blocked before deployment.
